Who am I, anyway?

Greetings, I am Aelthyna. You have stumbled on my own little corner of the web, serving as a sort of personal digital diary. Whatever it is I may feel like writing, be it an essay, a review, a project idea, or just a thought I felt like putting into writing, finds it way here.

My website's title is meant to be a nod to how I want this website to become a permanent repository of some of my reflections over time. Thoughts can be like flowing water; if you do not make the effort of catching them in time, they are gone forever.

I like all kinds of creative media, though I have not explored them all equally. I am especially keen on cinema, literature, music, and videogames, though I am always open to anything that can resonate with me—or, better yet, that I can resonate with.

If you found any of my work interesting, and you just feel like chatting with me, then please, by all means! I can be found on MyAnimeList, and my Discord handle is aelthyna.
