Backgrounds from Ghost in the Shell (1995)

While Masamune Shirow's original manga remains one of my favorite depictions of the Ghost in the Shell universe, Mamoru Oshii managed to imbue the story with an artistic dignity and philosophical depth it simply did not have before. Thoughtful, quiet, contemplative, melancholic; this film never fails to put me in a genuine trance in its moments of greatest reflection, and the backgrounds, together with the choral chants forming the film's soundtrack, play a significant role in that.
