Who am I, anyway?

Greetings, I am Aelthyna. You have stumbled on my own little corner of the web, serving as a sort of personal digital diary. Whatever it is I may feel like writing, be it an essay, a review, a project idea, or just a thought I felt like putting into writing, finds it way here.

My website's title is meant to be a nod to how I want this website to become a permanent repository of some of my reflections over time. Thoughts can be like flowing water; if you do not make the effort of catching them in time, they are gone forever.

One could argue the only knowledge that is really worth pursuing is that which pertains to us, as human beings. Part of it we derive from experiencing life directly, another—from experiencing the creative works of others. I believe that all creative works are fundamentally centered on humanity, on our experience and on a better understanding of our condition. In doing so, reflecting on them is just as essential as directly experiencing them. I hope that, with my writing, I may share some of this with the reader.

I like all kinds of creative media, though I have not explored them all equally. I am especially keen on cinema, literature, music, and videogames, though I am always open to anything that can resonate with me—or, better yet, that I can resonate with.

I do not believe in strictly distinguishing between "higher" and "lower" forms of creativity; I believe that the fundamental trait of a real creative work is honesty and a desire to explore humanity and the nature of its existence. As such, I believe that even "popular" art can be just as expressive, and worthy of consideration, as more "established" art.

If you found any of my work interesting, and you just feel like chatting with me, then please, by all means! I am always happy to meet a new person. I can be found on the following platforms, or you can just email me (although that is a little old-fashioned!) at aelthyna AT gmail DOT com.
